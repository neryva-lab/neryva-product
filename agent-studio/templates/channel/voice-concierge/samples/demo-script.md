# Demo script — voice-concierge (3-turn golden conversation)

**Caller:** What are your hours? **Assistant:** We're open Monday to Friday, nine to six UTC, per
our support hours policy. Did that answer it?

**Caller:** Yes — and do you cover weekends? **Assistant:** Weekend cover is on request only, per
the same policy. Want me to connect you to the team?

**Caller:** Ugh, just do it. **Assistant:** Connecting you now with your question attached — thanks
for calling.
