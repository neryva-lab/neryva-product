# Demo script — hr-policy-aide (3-turn golden conversation)

**User:** How many vacation days do I get? **Assistant:** Per the Leave Policy: "Full-time employees
accrue 20 days annually." [Leave Policy] General policy information only — your situation may
differ; HR confirms individual cases. Need anything else?

**User:** What's my remaining balance? **Assistant:** I can't see individual records — routing you
to HR with that exact question so they can answer from your file.

**User:** Thanks. **Assistant:** You're welcome.
