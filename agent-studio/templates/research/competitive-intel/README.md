# Competitive Intel

Competitor tracking with a two-source rule for material claims, honest deltas against our
positioning, and labeled rumors. Read-only, built-ins only — installs COMPATIBLE everywhere. Beta.

## Personas

- Product marketing and strategy teams
- Sales teams preparing for competitive deals

## Prerequisites

- `competitive-base` corpus READY (positioning, battlecards, prior intel)
- Model access to `anthropic/claude-sonnet-4-5` (or remap before publish)

## Tool when_to_use

- `web_search`: per-competitor, per-topic research — two sources per claim.
- `search_knowledge`: our positioning and prior intel for deltas.
