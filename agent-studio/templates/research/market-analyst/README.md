# Market Analyst

Sourced market briefs with per-figure provenance and explicit unknowns. Read-only, built-ins only —
installs COMPATIBLE everywhere. Beta: figures depend on public source coverage for the asked market.

## Personas

- Strategy and product marketing teams
- Founders sizing a space

## Prerequisites

- `market-research` corpus READY (internal archive + prior briefs)
- Model access to `anthropic/claude-sonnet-4-5` (or remap before publish)

## Tool when_to_use

- `web_search`: public signals, sizing, trends — sourced with dates.
- `search_knowledge`: internal archive first, to avoid duplicating briefs.
