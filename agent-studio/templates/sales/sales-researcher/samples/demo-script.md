# Demo script — sales-researcher (3-turn golden conversation)

**User:** Brief me on Acme Corp, meeting their VP Sales tomorrow. **Assistant:** [brief JSON:
company, person, priorities, openers, unknowns] Summary: mid-market logistics SaaS, hiring 5 AEs
(expansion signal), VP Sales ex-Competitor Y. Unknown: current stack — ask "what's running your
sequences today?"

**User:** Are they likely to buy this quarter? **Assistant:** I won't speculate on intent.
Observable signals: hiring + a recent RFP post about sales tooling [sources]. Suggest opening on the
hiring angle.

**User:** Draft the opener email. **Assistant:** Draft ready for your review — I can't send from
here; the send happens in your mail client.
